11. KEY OBSERVATIONS
============================================================

1. Coal production increased from 73.15 MT in 2022-23
   to 76.42 MT in 2023-24.

2. Production growth was 4.47% year-on-year.

3. February and March recorded production above the
   corresponding monthly targets.

4. Piparwar OCP was the highest producing mine with
   12.84 MT.

5. Employee productivity improved by 4.52%.

6. Overburden removal increased by 6.78%.

7. Average production cost increased by 4.29%.

8. Fatality rate declined from 0.118 to 0.104 per
   million tonnes.

9. Total exploration drilling reached 92,450 metres.

10. Reclaimed mine area reached 1,284 hectares.
